
typically the laser pulse gas a given duration and in the trajectory of the particles needs to be stored in a number of points per laser period. a typical value is NT = 100 points per laser period. if the electrons are initially at rest (or on average) the stepsize in the numerical integration of the equations of motion becomes 

$$dt_r = \frac{T}{NT} = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_0 NT}$$
however, if the electron bunch has an average ${\bf p}\ne0$ then the Doppler factor needs to be included, and the formula becomes 

$$dt_m = \frac{dt_r}{p^0-p_z},\qquad p^0 = \sqrt{(mc)^2+p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2}$$
