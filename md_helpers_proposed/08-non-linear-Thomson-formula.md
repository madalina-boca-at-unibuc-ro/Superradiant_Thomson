# Derivation of the non-linear Thomson formula for the radiation field 

the non linear thomson formula is 

$$ \omega_N = N \omega_0 \frac{n_L\cdot q}{n_s\cdot q}$$

where $q$ is the dressed electron momentum 

$$q= p + (mc)\frac{a_0^2}{4}n_L$$

where $n_L$ is the laser propagation direction 4-vector 

$$n_L=(1,0,0,1)$$

and $n_s$ is a four vecctor of the screen direction 

$$n_s=(1,0,0,1)$$ if $z_0>0$ and $$n_s=(1,0,0,-1)$$ if $z_0<0$, with $z_0$ the screen $Z$ coordinate.

the electron momentum $p$ is the mean momentum of the electron bunch 

$$p=(\sqrt{(mc)^2+ p_x^2+p_y^2+p_z^2}, p_x, p_y, p_z)$$
